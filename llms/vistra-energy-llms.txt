# Vistra Corp

> Vistra Corp (NYSE: VST) is an Irving, Texas integrated retail electricity and power generation company and the largest competitive power generator in the United States, operating roughly 41,000-44,000 MW of natural gas, nuclear, coal, solar and battery storage capacity and serving nearly 5 million retail customers across 16 states and the District of Columbia through TXU Energy, Dynegy, Ambit Energy, Energy Harbor, Homefield Energy and TriEagle Energy. Vistra publishes NO public API: no developer portal, no API host, and no machine-readable contract of any kind. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the catalog record at https://github.com/api-evangelist/vistra-energy, not published by Vistra.

## API posture (read this first)

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema, Postman collection or MCP server exists on any Vistra-controlled domain (probed 2026-07-27).
- developer., developers., docs., api. and data. on vistracorp.com do not resolve; /developers, /api, /docs, /data, /openapi.json, /swagger.json and /.well-known/* return 404.
- The only machine-readable surface served from vistracorp.com is the WordPress CMS REST API that backs the marketing site, which robots.txt disallows. It is infrastructure, not a product API, and is deliberately not catalogued.
- The only customer-data surface is the TXU Energy MyAccount web application (login required). Its interval readings come from Smart Meter Texas, the shared ERCOT meter-data platform operated by the Texas transmission and distribution utilities, not by Vistra.
- Homonym warning: devportal.vistra.com ("API Developer Portal") belongs to Vistra Limited, an unrelated corporate-services and fund-administration firm. It is NOT Vistra Corp the power company.

## Where the data actually lives (third parties, not Vistra)

- [Smart Meter Texas](https://smartmetertexas.com/): TDU-operated ERCOT meter-data platform where third-party interval-data access is registered in Texas.
- [US DOE Green Button](https://www.energy.gov/data/green-button): Names TXU Energy on the voluntary "Utilities Committed to Implementing Green Button" list. Commitment, not a verified implementation.
- ERCOT, PJM, ISO-NE, NYISO, MISO, CAISO, EIA and FERC publish the open market and generation data about Vistra's fleet. Vistra publishes none of its own.
